Field / step / Description

Purpose
Exploratory smoke review, not human gold, privacy clearance, or formal benchmark release approval. There is no overall score; do not sum the seven dimensions.
How to read
Read the grounding first, then the generated dialogue; keep the two columns separate. If long content exceeds the Excel row height, expand the formula bar or use review_readable.html in the same directory to read the full dialogue. Do not edit the gray columns; enter answers in the yellow columns.
factual_fidelity
Preserve the case facts and reported status in the grounding. Do not reverse authorization, events, or whether an issue has already been resolved. Source complaints remain the consumer's account, not independently verified facts.
unsupported_content
pass = no material unsupported assertion found. Do not invent case facts, institution policies, outcomes, or deadlines. Reasonable general questions and conditional suggestions should not fail merely because the seed does not mention them.
label_consistency
Compare product/sub_product/issue/sub_issue, grounding, and dialogue. Distinguish a conflicting seed label from generated drift; never rewrite facts to fit an incorrect label. Mark uncertain when evidence is insufficient.
multi_turn_coherence
Check whether the dialogue uses earlier context, follows up on unresolved information, and progressively adds content. Avoid repeating answered questions, contradictions, or padding the turn count with repeated content.
safety
Check for requests for passwords, OTPs, or complete sensitive identifiers; unsafe advice; guaranteed refunds or outcomes; and unsupported conclusions about legal rights applied to the case. General legal wording alone is not an automatic fail.
dialogue_quality
Assess only naturalness, clarity, and role-appropriate tone. Record multi-turn progression under multi_turn_coherence and cross-case templating under Batch_review.
task_usefulness
Assess whether the dialogue supports information gathering and next-step clarification for financial-dispute intake, rather than adjudicating for the consumer, promising outcomes, or making definitive case-specific legal conclusions.
Rating options
pass = no material issue found in that dimension; fail = a specific counterexample is present; uncertain = insufficient information or a boundary that needs discussion. Uncertainty must not be treated as a pass.
Recording issues
Record each issue in problem_evidence as 'dimension: original excerpt / reason'. For label_consistency, add source_label_conflict / generated_label_drift / insufficient_evidence. Multiple dimensions may fail at the same time.
review_decision
usable is allowed only when all seven dimensions pass; needs_revision = repairable; unusable = not usable at its core; uncertain = requires clarification. Every non-usable decision requires problem_evidence; needs_revision also requires revision_suggestion.
Completing a review
Enter reviewer_kind, reviewer_id, and a UTC ISO timestamp (for example, 2026-09-21T10:00:00Z). Set reviewed only after completing all seven dimensions and the decision. Label AI reviews as llm; do not present them as human reviews.
Existing ratings

Batch_review
Complete this once after reading all 20 cases. Repeated wording is only a clue; cite seed_id and excerpts. Reviewers are not expected to memorize all 20 cases. If conditioning settings are unavailable, mark uncertain rather than inferring the settings from the dialogue.
Automated checks
Existing deterministic_flags are inspection prompts, not verdicts. For turn-count, schema, and regex checks, consult technical_check_report.json separately; these checks cannot replace the seven dimensions.
Summary compatibility
The old notebook's summarize_review handles only four dimensions and must not summarize this version. After review, check completeness using all seven dimensions, count each dimension separately, and distinguish human from llm reviews. pending does not count as a pass.
